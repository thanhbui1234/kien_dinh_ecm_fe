# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

B2B industrial buyers in Vietnam — factory owners, procurement engineers, and technical decision-makers — researching CNC machine tools, cutting tools, and spare parts. They arrive evaluating supplier credibility (history, production facilities, past projects) before reaching out via contact/lead forms.

## Product Purpose

Thanh Bằng's public marketing site establishes the brand as a modern, credible CNC machine-tool and cutting-tool supplier in Vietnam. It showcases products, past projects, company history, and production facilities to build trust and drive B2B lead generation.

## Brand Personality

Modern — Precise — Dynamic. Confidently technical without being stiff or dated; industrial credibility expressed through clean structure and deliberate color, not decoration.

## Anti-references

- Dated industrial-supplier sites: dense gray data tables, clip-art icons, cluttered catalogs.
- Generic AI-SaaS cream/beige near-white landing pages — no personality, no technical edge.

## Design Principles

1. Precision reads as premium — confident whitespace and sharp typography over ornamentation.
2. One accent, used deliberately — `#5e8dd1` blue signals trust and technical precision; it does not get diluted across every element.
3. Section rhythm through tonal variation, not clutter — alternating background tones guide the eye between sections without dividers or side-stripes.
4. Vietnamese-first content, international credibility — typography and layout render Vietnamese diacritics cleanly at every scale.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA baseline: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast against any section background tint, large/bold text ≥3:1. All motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion` (already implemented in `globals.css`).
